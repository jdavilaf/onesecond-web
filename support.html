Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Follow the instruction shown for each challenge. You have up to one second to respond, and one mistake ends the run. Check the in-game guide for each challenge type.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings using the sliders icon on Home, then select REMOVE ADS. This is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. The purchase screen shows the price before you confirm.

I purchased Remove Ads, but ads still appear.

Open Settings → REMOVE ADS → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally made the purchase and check your connection. If the problem continues, contact support with the app version and the steps you tried.

Do I need Game Center?

No. You can play without signing in. Game Center enables Apple-hosted leaderboards and achievements when available. Manage your Game Center account in your iPhone Settings.

Where is my progress stored?

Local records, statistics and preferences are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app can remove this information. Game Center entries and Apple purchases are managed separately by Apple; do not assume local progress will transfer automatically to another device.

Can I play offline?

The core game works offline. Purchases, restoration, Game Center and advertising services require a connection.

Where can I change advertising privacy choices?

When available for your region, open Settings → PRIVACY CHOICES. Buying Remove Ads stops future in-app advertising, but does not erase information previously processed by advertising providers.

The game freezes or closes unexpectedly.

Close and reopen the app, restart your iPhone and check for app and iOS updates. If the issue persists, email support. Avoid deleting the app as a first step because local progress may be lost.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com. Support cannot issue an Apple refund directly.
